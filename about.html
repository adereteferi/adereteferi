Who Am I?

My name is Adere Teferi, and I am a second-year Information Technology student at Wolayta Sodo University (WSU). I am passionate about technology, learning new skills, and improving my knowledge in the field of IT. My academic journey has been full of growth, determination, and valuable experiences that continue to shape my future.

I completed my matriculation examination in 2015 according to the Ethiopian calendar. Passing the matric exam was an important milestone in my life because it opened the door to higher education and motivated me to work harder toward my goals and dreams.

After matriculation, I began my university education at Asosa University (ASU), where I studied for one year. During my time there, I gained academic experience, adapted to university life, and improved my confidence and independent learning skills.

Later, I transferred to Wolayta Sodo University to continue my studies in Information Technology. At WSU, I have continued to strengthen my skills in programming, networking, databases, and computer applications.

As a second-year student, I am focused on building a strong foundation for my future career. I enjoy exploring modern technologies and learning how technology can solve real-world problems. I strongly believe that education, discipline, and hard work are the keys to success.

My goal is to become a skilled IT professional who can contribute positively to society through technology. I also aim to improve my English communication skills, technical knowledge, and practical experience. I am proud of my journey so far and excited about the opportunities ahead in my academic and professional life.
